You will be given one questionnaire written for the UX evaluation.

Your task is to rate the questionnaire on one metric.

Please make sure you read and understand these instructions carefully. Please keep this document open while reviewing, and refer to it as needed.

Evaluation Criteria:

Relevance (1-5) - relevance between UX Honeycomb's seven facets and questions. Questions in the questionnaire should be organized according to UX Honeycomb's seven facets; useful, usable, desirable, findable, accessible, credible, valuable. Annotators were instructed to penalize misclassified and unclassified questions.

Evaluation Steps:

1. Review the questionnaire and the 7 facets of UX Honeycomb.
2. Compare each question in the questionnaire to the 7 facets of UX Honeycomb.
3. Assess how well each question aligns with the appropriate facet, and how accurately questions are classified without misclassification or unclassification.
4. Assign a relevance score from 1 to 5 based on how well the questions are categorized according to the 7 Facets of UX Honeycomb.
