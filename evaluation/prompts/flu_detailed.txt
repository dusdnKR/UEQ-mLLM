Evaluation Criteria:

Fluency (1-3): the quality of the questionnaire in terms of grammar, spelling, punctuation, word choice, and sentence structure. In particular, make sure that the sentence follows the correct case notation.

- 1: Poor. The questionnaire has many errors that make it hard to understand or sound unnatural.
- 2: Fair. The questionnaire has some errors that affect the clarity or smoothness of the text, but the main points are still comprehensible.
- 3: Good. The questionnaire has few or no errors and is easy to read and follow.
