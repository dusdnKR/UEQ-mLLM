You will be given one questionnaire written for the UX evaluation.

Your task is to rate the questionnaire on one metric.

Please make sure you read and understand these instructions carefully. Please keep this document open while reviewing, and refer to it as needed.

Evaluation Criteria:

Consistency (1-5) - The consistency of the questionnaire form. The questionnaire should follow the predefined structure with questions categorized into seven categories: Useful, Usable, Desirable, Findable, Accessible, Credible, and Valuable. The questionnaire must have at least five questions in each category, for a total of at least 35 questions.

- 5: Each category has at least 5 questions. All questions are under 7 categories.

- 1: Each item has fewer than 5 questions. There are questions entered separately outside the 7 categories.

Evaluation Steps:

1. Read the questionnaire carefully.
2. Compare each question to the questionnaire form: Ensure that each question is correctly categorized under one of the seven categories.
3. Check for categorization accuracy: Pay special attention to whether the questions are properly classified. If there are questions not categorized under any of the seven items, deduct points significantly.
4. Verify the question count: Ensure that there are at least 35 questions in total, with a minimum of five questions per category. If any category has fewer than five questions, subtract points accordingly.
5. Assign a consistency score from 1 to 5: Use the Evaluation Criteria to determine the score, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
